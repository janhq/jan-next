# Notice Patterns

Adapt these skeletons to the user's facts. Replace bracketed items with material from the user; write "To be confirmed" where the material is silent.

## Policy or procedure change

- SUBJECT: [Policy name]: [what changes] effective [date]
- Purpose: This notice announces [change] and the steps each [group] must take.
- Key facts: what is changing (old -> new), effective date, who is affected, basis for the decision.
- Required action: numbered steps with owner; transition arrangements for items already in progress.
- Deadline: date by which the action must be completed.
- Contact: owner of the policy, channel for exceptions.

## Deadline reminder or request for response

- SUBJECT: Action required by [date]: [what is requested]
- Purpose: One sentence stating the request and why it is needed.
- Key facts: what is requested, in which format or system, where to find the form or link.
- Required action: submit/confirm/approve, with the responsible group.
- Deadline: exact date and time; consequence of non-response only if sourced.
- Contact: who handles late or incomplete submissions.

## Service interruption or incident notice

- SUBJECT: [System or service]: [planned maintenance / outage] on [date, time window]
- Purpose: Tell readers what is unavailable, when, and what to do meanwhile.
- Key facts: affected systems, window with time zone, impact, workaround, expected restoration.
- Required action: what users should do before, during and after (save work, use backup channel).
- Deadline: preparation cut-off, if any.
- Contact: support desk with hours; escalation path.

## Event, meeting or training announcement

- SUBJECT: [Event name] on [date] at [place or link]
- Purpose: Invite or notify the audience and state whether attendance is required.
- Key facts: date, time with time zone, venue or link, agenda or topics, speakers, preparation materials.
- Required action: register/confirm attendance, bring items, submit questions.
- Deadline: registration closing date.
- Contact: organizer name and email.

## Organizational announcement (appointment, restructure, closure)

- SUBJECT: [Announcement]: [person or unit] [change], effective [date]
- Purpose: State the change in the first sentence; give context in the second.
- Key facts: who, what role or unit, effective date, reporting lines, handover arrangements.
- Required action: only if readers must do something (redirect requests, update records); otherwise say "No action is required."
- Deadline: transition end date, if any.
- Contact: who handles questions during the transition.

## Words that work

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| "ASAP", "soon" | the exact date and time |
| "Please be advised that" | state the fact directly |
| "It has come to our attention" | "We have found" or "[Team] found" |
| "Kindly revert" | "Please reply by [date]" |
| "Staff are requested to" | "All [group] must" (obligation) or "should" (advice) |
