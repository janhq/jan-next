# Official memo and notice: layout and usage notes

This is a fictional example of an organizational notice. The issuing office, notice number, readers and body text are not real. The template was built from a blank document and carries no content, images, metadata or fonts from any earlier example.

Layout: US Letter portrait with 1 inch margins. From top to bottom: an editable letterhead line in navy bold serif type, a notice-number and issue-date line, a navy divider rule, a centered bold title, a To / From block, an opening paragraph (purpose), numbered section headings with optional numbered sub-headings, body paragraphs, the signing office and date right-aligned, a cc table, and a centered "Page X of Y" footer with editable PAGE / NUMPAGES fields. The fonts are the standard Times New Roman (body, letterhead, title) and Arial (headings, labels, footer); none are embedded.

Content pattern for a new notice: state the purpose in the opening paragraph, then the key facts (what, when, where), then the action required of each reader with its deadline, then a contact for questions. Use short numbered sections; sub-sections are only for genuine sub-points. Keep the tone direct and neutral, and mark unknown facts as to be confirmed instead of inventing them.

After the host binds the current revision of `official-memo`, use `office_template_write` to replace the letterhead (the issuing organization), the notice number line, the title, the To and From lines, the body, the signing office and the date. The letterhead paragraph cannot be removed. In the cc table at the end you can delete a single cc recipient or the whole table: while other recipients remain only the named one is removed; removing the "cc:" label or the last recipient removes the row, and removing every row removes the table. New paragraphs reuse the style of the paragraph they are cloned from. Before `present`, the host checks that the divider, footer and page setup still match the template.
